READ ME OF SNII AND TERRESTRIAL ECOLOGY OF MEXICO

Jorge Soberon, August 2026

In this repository there are files related to the SNII of Mexico, in general, and also filtered to terrestrial ecologists, plus a file with the titles of papers between the S. XVIII and 1967, published by the IMERNAR (Instituto Nacional de Recursos Bioticos, Sanchez (1969) and the results of a Query to the Web of Science database, performed by J. Soberon in 2025.
